What is the Purpose of IS-Match?

IS-Match is an authoritative sovereign decision-support platform established under the Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution in institutional coordination with the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) and the Government e-Marketplace (GeM). Its statutory purpose is to empower procurement officials across Central Ministries, Defence Establishments, State Governments, and PSUs to automatically identify, cross-reference, and mandate applicable Indian Standards (BIS) and Central Quality Control Orders (QCOs) in tender specifications—ensuring zero sub-standard procurement, total legal compliance under Section 16 of the BIS Act, 2016, and national safety assurance.

1. Solving Technical Specification Ambiguity:

Tender Inviting Authorities (TIAs) often specify generic or outdated product descriptions, leading to vendor disputes or procurement of sub-standard products. IS-Match bridges this gap by automatically converting natural language requirements into exact, legally enforceable Indian Standards.

2. Enforcing Central Quality Control Orders (QCOs):

Under Section 16 of the Bureau of Indian Standards Act, 2016, products covered under mandatory QCOs cannot be sold, stored, or distributed in India without valid BIS certification (ISI Mark / CRS). IS-Match alerts officials before tender publication to mandate BIS licenses, preventing illegal supply.

3. Seamless GeM & CPPP Clause Generation:

Provides copy-paste and downloadable tender technical schedules formatted to GeM Pre-Qualification Criteria (PQC), citing latest reaffirmed editions and published amendments.

How does IS-Match recommend Indian Standards?

The platform utilizes a semantic recommendation engine trained on the Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) technical database. It parses material composition, operating limits, and technical keywords to match exact standards, active amendments, and testing references.

Does IS-Match certify vendors or issue licenses?

No. The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the sole statutory certifying body. IS-Match references open public Gazette data to advise officials on statutory tender requirements and enables vendors to link existing valid licenses.

Can procurement officials download ready tender documents?

Yes. In the Procurement Portal (procurement.html), officials can generate and download a complete Notice Inviting Tender (NIT) schedule pack with official TIA credentials and GeM-ready technical clauses.
