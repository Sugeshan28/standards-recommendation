Mandatory BIS Certification Advisory for Procurement Officials:

Why certification matters: Procurement officials must determine before publishing a tender whether the product legally requires mandatory BIS certification under Central Government Quality Control Orders (QCOs). If not explicitly mandated in the tender, vendors could legally supply uncertified (illegal-to-sell) products, leading to audit objections, delivery rejections, or compromised safety.

Note: The Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS) is the sole statutory certifying authority. This engine identifies official public Gazette mandates to ensure your tender clauses are legally airtight.
